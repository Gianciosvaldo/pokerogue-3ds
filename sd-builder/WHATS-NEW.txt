What is in this build of PokeRogue 3DS
======================================

The exact version is in files\VERSION.txt, and the builder prints it when it starts and writes it to
build-log.txt. Quote it in a bug report.

Converted files: nothing new in the packs since the October 26 builder (same pictures, sounds and text), so extracting
this zip over the old folder and running it only replaces the game app: nothing is downloaded or converted again.
Coming from the October 24 builder or earlier, the first run converts everything again (up to half an hour).

New since the October 26 builder
  Mystery encounters (each one checked against the upstream game's own numbers)
    - Ported properly: Fight or Flight, Slumbering Snorlax, Dark Deal, Training Session and Berries Abound. The
      other kinds are still simplified stand-ins.
    - The question screen now draws each encounter's own picture (the bush, the crate, the scientist, the item
      icons...). Pokemon and trainers the encounter picks at random are not drawn there yet.
    - Training Session lets you pick the Pokemon, then a nature (25, "(new)" marks the ones not in the Pokedex yet)
      or an ability. Seen only in tests, not on a screen.
    - Dialogue fixes: encounter texts load again (they showed "Option 1" and blank screens).
  Football Playoff
    - The CPU varies its plays and reads the field; punts, field goals, kickoffs, extra points, two-point tries
      and returns; new animations for jukes, dives, tackles, sacks, interceptions, fumbles and touchdowns; the
      menus look like the Classic ones; species candy for plays; evolution items in the shop; members gain
      friendship and ten levels for each won round, and keep the move an evolution needs.
  Battle
    - Boss info box, HP bar dividers and shields are drawn.
    - In doubles the foes' platform goes up with the foes (Only the geometry was set; tell me what to move).
    - The Pokemon's sprites get first claim on texture memory (the blue/orange box on a Tentacruel). If you still see
      it, send the "free N" MB number on the bottom screen's debug line during that battle.
    - Fused enemies keep their identity and the secondary species can evolve after fusion level ups; long turns no
      longer drop events.
  Not checked on a real 3DS
    - Everything above. In particular the intro pictures' placement on the question screen and the Training pickers.

New in the October 26 builder (still in this one)
  Rivals and trainers
    - The six Classic rival waves are real fights now (a male player meets Ivy; the app has no player
      gender yet). Trainers speak their upstream encounter and victory lines in battle.
    - Custom runs: on each eligible trainer wave there is a 1 in 4 chance of a JoJo challenger instead
      (17 characters; each at most once a run, at most one per wave). Their sprites are fan art that ships
      inside this builder. Classic runs never meet them.
    - Custom runs also have a "Pillar Men" mystery encounter with three options: Esidisi (easy), Wamuu
      (hard) and Kars (brutal).
  Mystery encounters
    - All 20 kinds can come up in runs now (a bug had limited runs to 2 kinds), and they show a question
      screen with the upstream options. Several are still simplified stand-ins, the screen has no closing
      text yet, and the GTS second pick takes the first Pokemon in the list.
  Screens and layout
    - Starter select has upstream-style filters.
    - Move animations cover the whole top screen, background and graphics.
    - Pokemon and platforms are bigger on the top screen, and in doubles the foes sit clear of your boxes.
      Only the geometry was set, nothing was seen on a screen: tell me what to move.
  Achievements
    - 68 of about 100, on the title menu, with their rewards: 30 for the JoJo challengers, 14 about runs,
      16 about the collection and 8 about eggs. Not in yet: the 14 battle feats, the mystery encounter and
      economy ones, and the rival and party ones.
  Fixes
    - A run saved after Counter, Mirror Coat or Metal Burst loads again.
    - Fights inside a mystery encounter no longer overwrite the run's one save.
    - Exact battle history for Protect streaks, Last Resort, Conversion 2, Lock-On and more; Macho Brace;
      held form items and their Check Team actions.

Earlier builds, still in this one
  - Football Playoff on the title menu: its own save, playcalling, progression and shops.
  - Starter customization: select a Pokemon, then Customize to choose moves, an unlocked nature, shiny tier
    or ability. Use candies buys the passive or hidden ability at the species passive price, and an unlocked
    passive can be switched on or off. Caught natures and mints stay unlocked. Catching, hatching and
    friendship give species candy. Team luck shows before a run and in the battle commands.
  - Eggs show a cracking and reveal animation. Customize > Use candies > Buy species egg buys an egg of that
    Pokemon. Eggs unlock starters after enough victories; they do not join your party.
  - Doubles: Lures lead to playable double battles. Choose a move for each ally and a target when asked;
    B on the second ally undoes the first. Hold SELECT in battle for upstream's stat-change panels.
  - Hatch results show the IV radar, nature and new egg moves. Three starters get Pokerus every three hours.
    Customize > Use candies offers two starter cost reductions. Fight shows consecutive-move power.
  - IV Scanner questions appear when you enter an ordinary wild battle with the item. Hold SELECT on a shop
    TM to read its move's effect. Existing PP Ups show in item menus.
  - Your trainer throws the opening Poke Ball and the rivals are visible. The run report can name the
    final blow.

Not checked on a real 3DS
  Automated checks on a PC cannot replace your console. In particular: the one-file PokeRogue.3dsx and
  PokeRogue.cia (the packs inside the file) have never been started on a console; the new top-screen
  sizes, the mystery encounter screen, the Pillar Men options and the achievements screen have not been
  seen on any screen; and Valentine's quote is long enough that it may wrap badly in the dialogue box.
  If PokeRogue.3dsx will not start, choose answer 3 (loose files) and tell the project thread what the
  3DS screen says.
