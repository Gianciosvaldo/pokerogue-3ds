PokeRogue SD card builder (Windows 10 or 11)
=============================================

October 24 battle fixes and football build
  Includes merged battle fixes through PR #396 and Football Playoff on the title
  menu, with its own save/resume file, playcalling, progression and shops.
  Battle-core completion is still in progress. This refresh also reduces reward
  dispatcher stack use on the 3DS. The app revision is in files\VERSION.txt.

Earlier hardware and starter changes included in this build
  Includes the battle-menu crash/TM shop fixes, corrected move names, battle stat
  stage display, egg hatching, IV Scanner prompts, visible rivals and your trainer's
  opening Poke Ball throw. The run report can name an engine-recorded final blow.
  Existing game speed settings are included.
  Build a fresh game file and replace the older PokeRogue.3dsx on your card. The
  builder's files\VERSION.txt identifies the app revision for a bug report.
  Please check the opening throw and rivals, TM compatibility/cancel, and Fight/Ball
  menus. Eggs unlock starters after enough victories; they do not join your party.
  IV Scanner questions appear when entering an ordinary wild battle with the item.
  Hold SELECT during battle to see upstream's stat-change panels over all active
  Pokemon's health boxes, including both opponents in a double battle. Release
  SELECT to hide them. Lures now lead to playable double battles: choose a move
  for each active ally and choose a target when prompted (B goes back).
  Eggs now show a cracking/reveal animation with the hatched Pokemon's sprite.
  Customize > Use candies > Buy species egg buys an egg of that Pokemon.
  B on the second ally's command menu now undoes the first ally's selection.
  Hold SELECT while hovering over a shop TM to read the move's effect.
  Existing PP Ups appear in item menus. Successful free TM teaching consumes
  that reward; cancelling lets you choose again. Level-up stats use the bottom
  screen, and ability descriptions scroll independently of battle speed.
  Hatch results show a neutral background, IV radar, nature and new egg moves.
  Claimed Nemeses leave the available list. Doubles bars sit further outward,
  sprite loading avoids duplicate atlases, and returning to singles clears allies.
  Three starters receive Pokerus every three hours. Customize > Use candies also
  offers two starter cost reductions. Consecutive-move power is shown in Fight.
  Automated checks cannot replace testing on your physical 3DS.

Before starting a run
  Select a Pokemon, then Customize: choose moves, an unlocked nature, shiny tier
  or ability. Use candies buys its passive or hidden ability at the species passive
  price. You can toggle an unlocked passive. Choices are saved in your profile.
  Species with no hidden ability have no hidden-ability purchase option.
  Mints and caught natures stay unlocked. Catching, hatching and friendship earn
  species candy. Team luck is shown before the run and in battle commands.

What this does
  The 3DS game needs PokeRogue's pictures, sounds, music and text, converted for the 3DS
  (about 850 MB of files). Nobody is allowed to share those, so this tool makes them on
  your own PC from PokeRogue's public files and puts them on your SD card, inside the game
  itself: you get ONE file, PokeRogue.3dsx (or PokeRogue.cia if you'd rather install it).

What you need
  - This PC, connected to the internet.
  - Your 3DS's SD card, in the PC (card slot or card reader). You can also do without it
    and copy the files later.
  - About 4 GB free on the PC (the one-file game is made on the PC first), and about 1 GB
    free on the SD card (about 2 GB if you choose the installable .cia).
  - 20 to 60 minutes, mostly waiting. You can use the PC meanwhile.

Steps
  1. Double-click BUILD-SD-CARD (in this folder).
     If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     If a box asks "Do you want to run this file?", click "Run".
     These boxes appear because this tool isn't signed by a company.
  2. A black window opens. It looks for your SD card and shows its letter (for example E:).
     Press Enter to use it. If it can't find the card, type the card's letter and press Enter.
  3. It asks how you want the game on your 3DS. Press Enter for 1 (PokeRogue.3dsx) unless
     you know you want the installable one:
       1  PokeRogue.3dsx   one file in the 3ds folder; start it from the Homebrew Launcher
                          like your other apps. Try this one first.
       2  PokeRogue.cia    one file in the cias folder; install it once with FBI, then start
                          it from the HOME menu. Needs custom firmware with signature
                          patches (Luma3DS) and FBI, which you probably have already.
       3  Loose files      the app plus the pack files in 3ds\pokerogue, how the earlier
                          builds worked. Use this if 1 doesn't start.
     (Want both 1 and 2? Run the builder twice, once with each answer. The second run
     doesn't download or convert anything again.)
  4. Wait. It downloads about 700 MB, converts everything, makes the game file, and copies it
     to the SD card.
     It says "Done" at the end. If it stops or you close it, double-click BUILD-SD-CARD
     again: it carries on where it stopped.
  5. If it says one more step is needed for sound, do the three short steps it shows
     (they run a small tool called DSP1 once on the 3DS).
  6. Eject the SD card before taking it out: in File Explorer, right-click the card's drive
     and choose Eject. Put the card back in your 3DS.

If something goes wrong
  The window says what happened and what to do. If it keeps failing, send the file
  build-log.txt (it appears in this folder) in the project thread.

When you're done
  You can delete this whole folder to get the space back. Keep it if you'd rather not
  download everything again next time the game needs new files.

Saves
  Your saves stay in the 3ds\pokerogue\save folder on the SD card, whichever of the three you
  use. Don't delete that folder. After a one-file build, the five .pak files that the old
  builds put in 3ds\pokerogue can be deleted to get 850 MB back.

Not tried on a real 3DS yet
  The one-file versions are checked on a PC and in an emulator, not on a real console.
  If PokeRogue.3dsx doesn't start, tell the project thread what the 3DS screen says, and
  use answer 3 meanwhile.

Please keep the files it makes to yourself: they're only for your own console.
