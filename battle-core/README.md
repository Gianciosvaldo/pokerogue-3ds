# battle-core: native C port of PokeRogue's battle logic

Status 2026-09-27: **damage path with abilities and passives, move effects, held items, battler and arena tags, and the turn history verified against upstream** (singles); the turn engine has started and replays about 82% of the plain random test turns exactly, with stat changes, statuses, on-hit abilities, two-turn moves, the common move-added battler tags, recoil, drain, recharge, pre-move texts, the healing phase, weather and terrain over a turn, the damaging traps, and Leech Seed, Substitute, the protections and Endure ported (see "Turn engine"). Every damaging move in the random test is now covered except 6 cases in about 110,000. Upstream is `pagefaultgames/pokerogue` @ `8555c08`.

"Covered" means the C code hit nothing unported (see below). Every case also checks both Pokemon's stats, the attacker's hit counters after the calls, and the Super Fang state carried from case to case. The last three columns come from the test itself: `build/difftest` replays every passing case from its starting state without that piece of state (no battler or arena tags; no passives; empty move histories and received attacks and no turn order) and counts the answers that change. 81 cases are left out because upstream itself throws on them: Beat Up with inconsistent hit counters (38), a random state no real battle reaches, and Fusion Flare or Bolt when a Pokemon in the last turn order has no history entry at all (43), where upstream reads `.result` of the missing entry; the port reports that one as unported rather than guess. The mutation counts below for items, tags and passives were measured on the previous revision's case streams (adding the turn history changed the random streams). A mutation check confirms the test notices when the code is wrong: changing any one of terrain power, the Stellar boost, Payback, Lash Out, Retaliate, weight power, Magnitude, Trump Card, friendship power, Freeze-Dry or Psyshock produces mismatches, and so does each of 19 deliberate item bugs (for example vitamins at +11%, Soul Dew's sign, Eviolite on a final evolution, Leek on any species, Knock Off counting untransferable items, Acrobatics counting virtual stacks, id -1 items ignored, and computing the damage tokens with the C library's `pow`, which alone gives 15 wrong damages in 30,000 cases). Of 43 deliberate tag bugs, 41 produce mismatches across the four seeds (screens on the wrong side, on crits or at x1, sports at x0.5, Lucky Chant on the attacker's side, Neutralizing Gas off or ignoring unsuppressable abilities, Charge at x1.5, Supreme Overlord at 20%, Focus Energy at 1 stage, Protosynthesis boosting every stat, Autotomize at 10 kg, Spit Up off, Smack Down off...). Some are caught by only a handful of cases, so they are thinly tested: a type boost matching the move's base type instead of its final type (2 cases), Spit Up with 0 stacks (2), Unburden (5), Substitute blocking contact (6), Disguise behind a Substitute (13) and Infiltrator going through screens (22). The 2 not caught: a sound move going through a Substitute (no case combined a sound move, a Substitute and an effect that depends on whether the move hits it), and Sturdy skipping its tag (a side effect nobody compares). Of 34 deliberate bugs in the passive, party, move-queue, Present, Super Fang, Parental Bond and priority code, 31 are caught, from Beat Up without its +5 (444 cases) to Gale Wings reading the move's base type (2) and Disguise's recoil put off (2). The 3 not caught: Prankster switched off and Parental Bond accepting spread moves, which can't change a damaging move's result in a single battle, and a passive Long Reach being ignored by `hasAbilityWithAttr` (no case met it with a contact-dependent effect). All 25 deliberate turn-history bugs, measured on this revision's four runs, are caught, from Rollout counting the move being used (629 cases) and Counter taking the oldest attack instead of the newest (382) to Rollout never ignoring its limit (17), `areAllies` treating ATTACKER as a player slot (18) and Fusion Flare stopping at a failed move instead of passing over it (7, the thinnest). Others: Rollout without its reset on failure (181) or at the limit (42), Defense Curl's combo ignored (126), Echoed Voice doubling (50), Stomping Tantrum reading the newest move (210) or not counting failures (71), Revenge ignoring the attacker (98) or 0 damage (41), Fusion Flare counting the mover itself (41), walking the turn order oldest first (40) or ignoring the partner's result (150), Counter counting allies (232) or status moves (34), ignoring the category (154) or filtering Metal Burst (139), and the history keeping the wrong entry when full (43).

The differential test found real bugs, all fixed: Stellar Tera typing, Shedinja's forced Bug tera type, charging moves (Fly, Dive) not counting as attacks for Wonder Guard, and Disguise's recoil, which upstream deals in the middle of the damage calculation: when it knocks the Pokemon out, a passive checked right after (Fluffy, say) no longer applies. The port had put the recoil off until later; it now happens at once. Until this revision it also skipped every move whose listed power is -1 (35 moves, among them Low Kick, Grass Knot, Heavy Slam, Flail, Gyro Ball, Electro Ball, Punishment, Seismic Toss and Super Fang), so their code was unchecked even though an earlier version of this README counted them as verified. They're in the test now and match.

What the test doesn't cover yet: doubles and fusions. Code that is ported but can't show in this test: status moves hitting a Substitute and Prankster's priority (the test only uses damaging moves, and Prankster only speeds up status moves), Parental Bond's refusal of spread moves (no damaging move has two targets in a single battle), and the condition that turns off Color Change, Pickpocket, Berserk, Anger Shell, Wimp Out and Emergency Exit after a Sheer Force hit (it reads the attacks received and runs, since `hasAbilityWithAttr` and co. ask `canApplyAbility` before looking at the attributes, but none of those abilities has an attribute the damage path reads: 3 deliberate bugs in it change no answer). Side effects that belong to the turn engine (Volt Absorb healing, Sap Sipper's stat raise, Sturdy's tag, Disguise's form change, Present's heal, the stat boosts a boss gets when damage breaks one of its segments) are recorded as *deferred*: the damage result is checked, the side effect isn't applied until the phase engine exists, and nothing compares the deferred list with upstream (so Sturdy skipping its tag when it already has one is ported but unchecked). Of about 110,000 cases 6 stay uncovered: 4 where Disguise's recoil, which upstream deals in the middle of the damage calculation, lands on a Pokemon with Wimp Out or Emergency Exit (`PostDamageAbAttr`: the switch-out belongs to the turn engine), and 2 where Revenge or a Counter-like move scans more received attacks than the 8 kept without finding its answer. Parental Bond's second hit with Expanding Force or Tera Starstorm, whose target can change (`VariableTargetAttr`), is still unported too; it didn't come up in this revision's streams. The game-mode rules around passives (no passive for the classic final boss or endless bosses, the passives challenge, daily-run passives) aren't modelled: they belong to the mode layer.

Two things upstream does that a reader might not expect, both kept: `getAttackDamage` doesn't cap damage at the target's HP, and with the enemy's damage tokens (up to 999 stacks of x1.05 in endless mode) it can exceed 2^63, so damage is a double in the C port too. And the JS `Math.pow` result depends on the engine: the reference here is Node 22 (V8 12.4), whose pow is fdlibm's; newer V8 versions switched to a correctly rounded pow, so upstream itself can deal 1 point more or less on a different browser.

## Turn engine (in progress, 2026-09-27)
`src/engine.c` ports upstream's phase queue: the `PhaseTree` levels, deferral, and the dynamic speed-ordered queues with their markers (MovePhase's own queue included), so a phase lands where upstream would put it even when it only shows something. `src/phases.c` ports the phases of a turn: TurnStart, Move, MoveEffect, MoveEnd, Faint, CheckInterlude, PositionalTag, Berry, CheckStatusEffect, PostTurnStatusEffect, TurnEnd, ResetStatus, StatStageChange, ObtainStatusEffect and MoveCharge, plus the message, animation and ability-display phases. A phase with no port yet is still created where upstream creates it, and the turn is reported unported. `src/move_effects.c` has the gates in front of a move's secondary effects (the effect chance and its roll), the stat changes moves make and the statuses they inflict. `src/closures_conditions.c` has the moves' own failure conditions (`move.applyConditions`): 70 of the 83 distinct sources, including Protect's consecutive-use odds, Fake Out, the weather and terrain moves, OHKO moves with Sturdy, screens, heal moves, Counter, Disable, Last Resort, Destiny Bond, Damp and Teleport. Not ported: the random-move callers (Metronome, Assist...), Encore, Wish and Future Sight, Transform, Sketch, Instruct, Mirror Move, Psycho Shift, Rest, Belch and Stuff Cheeks, Shell Trap and Revival Blessing. Some ported ones still report themselves unported in a state they don't model: the switch-out check of Roar, Whirlwind, Teleport, Baton Pass and Shed Tail, Chilly Reception in snow, an entry hazard already down, Reflect Type on a typeless target, Destiny Bond on wave 200 or a multiple of 250 (the game mode decides), and Rototiller with a bench.

The stat-change phase is complete as upstream has it: Simple and Contrary, Mist (and Infiltrator getting through it), the abilities that stop drops (Clear Body, White Smoke, Full Metal Body, Hyper Cutter, Keen Eye, Big Pecks, Illuminate, Mind's Eye, Flower Veil on the user's side), Mirror Armor reflecting drops back, raises and drops split into two phases, the clamp to ±6 and its messages, Opportunist copying raises, Defiant and Competitive. The White Herb check only reports itself (held items aren't in the turn test yet). The move conditions on stat changes are ported too (Rototiller, Flower Shield, Venom Drench, Magnetic Flux, Gear Up, Tera Blast's Stellar drop). Arena tags now count down at the end of the turn and end with their message; the four whose lapse or removal does more (Imprison, the Fire/Grass Pledge sea of fire, pending heals, Neutralizing Gas ending) report themselves unported. Status conditions are complete for moves that inflict them (the 86 moves with `StatusEffectAttr`, Tri Attack and Dire Claw picking theirs with the global RNG before the chance roll): `canSetStatus` with an existing or pending status, Misty Terrain on a grounded target, the type immunities (Poison and Steel unless the source has Corrosion, Electric, Ice or sun for freezing, Fire, Electric Terrain for sleep), the Pokemon's own abilities (Limber, Insomnia, Water Veil, Leaf Guard, Comatose, Shields Down in Meteor Form...), its side's (Sweet Veil, Pastel Veil, Flower Veil) and Safeguard with Infiltrator; then the ObtainStatusEffectPhase with its sleep length roll and Synchronize. Over a turn: sleep counting down (Early Bird, Sleep Talk and Snore), freezing with the one-in-four thaw and the moves that thaw their user (Burn Up only for a Fire type), full paralysis one time in eight, and poison, bad poison and burn damage at the end of the turn (Magic Guard, Poison Heal blocking it, Heatproof). A Pokemon with a cure-on-entry ability (Limber, Insomnia, Water Veil...) that has the status anyway is cured after being hit. Upstream keeps the last status each of those abilities cured on the ability object itself, for the whole session, and the cure message only appears once that is set; the C keeps the same session-wide table (`pr_status_healed_*`), and the test writes it with the state. Not ported yet: Rest, Psycho Shift, Toxic Spikes, Yawn, and falling asleep while semi-invulnerable (it removes a battler tag).

Abilities that react to a hit run where upstream runs them, after each hit (`applyOnGetHitAbEffects`): the defender's PostDefend abilities, Anger Point on a critical hit, the after-hit tag lapse, then the attacker's PostAttack abilities. Ported with their effects: Static, Flame Body, Poison Point and Effect Spore (with upstream's two separate status picks, one when checking and one when applying, and Effect Spore's Grass and Overcoat immunity), Rough Skin and Iron Barbs (Magic Guard, indirect damage, counted in the damage taken), the stat ones (Justified, Rattled, Weak Armor, Gooey, Tangling Hair, Stamina, Water Compaction, Steam Engine, Thermal Exchange, and Cotton Down on every other Pokemon on the field), Berserk and Anger Shell (this hit's damage taking the HP across half), Anger Point, Color Change, Poison Touch and Toxic Chain. The ones that add a battler tag, weather, terrain or an entry hazard, swap or give an ability, steal an item or turn a drain into damage (Cursed Body, Cute Charm, Perish Body, Wandering Spirit, Mummy, Sand Spit, Toxic Debris, Wind Power, Electromorphosis, Seed Sower, Pickpocket, Liquid Ooze, Stench, Magician) check their conditions and roll their chance as upstream does, so the random stream stays in step, and report the turn unported only when they would act.

Battler tags are now added, lapsed and removed as upstream does (`src/battler_tags.c`: `addTag` with the tag immunity abilities, `lapseTag`, `lapseTags`, `removeTag`), for the tag types whose own methods are ported: CHARGING, the four semi-invulnerable ones (FLYING, UNDERGROUND, UNDERWATER, HIDDEN), and now the ones moves add most often: FLINCHED (the move cancelled before it starts, Steadfast's Speed raise, Inner Focus), CONFUSED (Own Tempo, the 2 to 5 turns, the one-in-three hit on itself with 40 power from the effective Attack and Defense, which cancels the move), FRENZY (Outrage, Thrash, Petal Dance and Raging Fury locking their user in: the next use queued without PP while the move keeps succeeding, confusion when the rampage runs its course), TRAPPED and NO_RETREAT (not on Ghost types, not twice, and gone when the Pokemon that set it faints), MINIMIZED, CHARGED (used up by the next Electric move), TAR_SHOT (not on a Terastallized Pokemon), AUTOTOMIZED (stacking) and Glaive Rush's two tags. Misty Terrain blocking confusion reports itself unported (terrain turns are). Trapping moves that deal damage (Bind, Wrap, Fire Spin...) still report their tag types unported.

The healing phase (PokemonHealPhase) is ported: nothing for a fainted Pokemon or one off the field, Heal Block's message instead of the heal, the "HP is full" message, Healing Charm reporting itself (modifiers), the cap at max HP, a negative heal turning into damage, and the status and PP options. It heals the user of a drain move (half the damage of the hit, or the target's Attack for Strength Sap; nothing against Liquid Ooze, whose own reversal still reports itself), a Pokemon absorbing a move with Volt Absorb, Water Absorb and co. (a quarter of its max HP), and a Poisoned Pokemon with Poison Heal at the end of the turn (an eighth). Recoil is ported (Double-Edge, Brave Bird, Head Smash, Struggle and Chloroblast from max HP unless the move failed or missed; Rock Head and Magic Guard; the recoil counts as damage taken), and so is recharging: Hyper Beam and co. add RECHARGING with its placeholder in the move queue, and next turn the Pokemon's move is cancelled with its message. The player's recharge turn commands MoveId.NONE; the port used to take that for no command at all, which the heal test caught. Pre-move texts are ported too (Struggle's and Magnitude's always show, Fickle Beam's when its seeded roll doubles the power, Poltergeist's when the target holds a transferable item, Chilly Reception's unless another move called it); before that, every Struggle turn fell out of coverage. The dump now records the lapse types every tag type is built with, so a lapse only reports the unported tags it would actually reach, by name ("(tag FLINCHED)"). Two-turn moves work on both turns: MoveChargePhase shows the charge text, runs the move's charge effects (the dump now carries each move's `chargeAttrs`: Fly and co. going semi-invulnerable, Skull Bash, Meteor Beam and Electro Shot raising a stat), adds CHARGING, queues the move for next turn and writes the OTHER history entry; on the next turn the queued move runs without PP, CHARGING lapses in the MovePhase and the semi-invulnerable tag in the MoveEffectPhase, and moves aimed at a semi-invulnerable Pokemon miss unless they reach it (Thunder, Earthquake, Surf...) or No Guard is out. Solar Beam and Solar Blade firing at once in sun, and Electro Shot in rain, are now checked by the weather runs. Not ported: Smack Down and Thousand Arrows grounding a flying Pokemon (FallDownAttr adds tags of other types), Cramorant catching something on Dive, and falling asleep while semi-invulnerable.

Weather and terrain now run over a turn. The weather phase (WeatherEffectPhase) is ported: Cloud Nine and Air Lock suppress it; Sandstorm and Hail take a sixteenth of max HP from every Pokemon that isn't Ground, Rock or Steel (Ice for Hail), going by the Tera type, or the original types for a Stellar Tera; Sand Veil, Snow Cloak, Ice Body, Overcoat, Sand Rush, Sand Force and Magic Guard block it, and so does being underground or underwater. The weather abilities follow (Rain Dish, Dry Skin and Ice Body heal, Dry Skin and Solar Power hurt). At the end of the turn the weather and the terrain count down (the primal weathers, and a weather set with no turn count, never end); when one ends its message shows, Protosynthesis's or Quark Drive's boost goes and the abilities that react to the change are told. Grassy Terrain heals grounded Pokemon a sixteenth at the end of the turn. Harsh Sun stops Water attacks and Heavy Rain Fire attacks, going by the type after abilities and moves change it (Normalize, Liquid Voice, Hidden Power, Tera Blast...), and Misty Terrain keeps grounded Pokemon from being confused, with its message only for status moves. Not ported: moves and abilities that set or clear a weather or terrain (Rain Dance, Sunny Day, Drizzle, Sand Stream, Electric Surge, Ice Spinner...; the weather runs leave them out), weather-based form and type changes (Forecast, Flower Gift, Ice Face and Mimicry report themselves unported) and Hydration (an end-of-turn ability, like the others still missing).

The damaging traps are ported: Bind, Wrap, Fire Spin, Whirlpool, Clamp, Sand Tomb, Magma Storm, Snap Trap, Thunder Cage and Infestation last 4 or 5 turns, can't be added to a Pokemon that is already trapped or behind a Substitute (a Ghost type can be caught, unlike with Mean Look), take an eighth of max HP at the end of each turn but the last (Magic Guard blocks it), and end when the trapper leaves the field. Rapid Spin and Mortal Spin free the user (of Leech Seed too), Defog, Ice Spinner and Steel Roller clear a terrain (Defog also fog), and Tidy Up's Substitute removal runs. Clearing entry hazards, screens, Mist or Safeguard (Rapid Spin, Mortal Spin, Defog, Tidy Up, Brick Break, Psychic Fangs, Raging Bull, Feint) reports itself only when it finds such a tag, since removing an arena tag isn't ported; which side it looks at is. A trapped Pokemon not being able to switch out waits for switching.

Leech Seed, Substitute, the protections and Endure are ported. Leech Seed can't seed a Grass type; at the end of each turn the seeded Pokemon loses an eighth of its max HP (Magic Guard blocks it) and the opponent standing where the seeder stood gets it back, or loses it against Liquid Ooze (which counts even when the ability can't apply, as upstream checks it). Substitute and Shed Tail take their HP cost (Shed Tail rounds up) and make a doll with a quarter of the user's max HP, which frees the user from damaging traps. A hit the doll takes comes off the doll (the attacker's damage dealt counts at most what the doll had left), the first strike says so, and the doll breaks at the end of the move once its HP is gone. The doll's animations are phases upstream (PokemonAnimPhase: made, the holder coming out for its own move and going back, broken) and run in the same order, including the broken-doll animation being skipped when the holder's own move removes it (Tidy Up). Protect, Detect, Spiky Shield, King's Shield, Baneful Bunker, Burning Bulwark, Obstruct and Silk Trap stop a move when the hit check reaches them (a status move only for Protect, Detect, Spiky Shield and Baneful Bunker), end a multi-hit move (the target leaves the move's target list, which the command and the move history share), and punish contact: Spiky Shield takes an eighth of the attacker's max HP unless the protected Pokemon (not the attacker, as upstream has it) has Magic Guard, Baneful Bunker poisons, Burning Bulwark burns, and King's Shield, Obstruct and Silk Trap lower Attack, Defense (by 2) or Speed. Endure keeps its user at 1 HP, with its message. The protections that guard a whole side (Quick Guard, Wide Guard, Mat Block, Crafty Shield) are arena tags and still report themselves, as do Sturdy's and the Endure token's survival.

## Design (the decisions that make 180k lines tractable)
1. **Data is generated, never hand-typed.** Upstream builds moves and abilities from ~430 attribute classes (`StatStageChangeAttr`, `StatMultiplierAbAttr`...). The dumper serialises every instance with its parameters. `gen/gen_tables.py` emits one C struct per class, a const table of instances, and an is-a bitmap for the class hierarchy. Upstream's `applyAbAttrs("X")` becomes "iterate attrs where `pr_attr_is(cls, PR_AC_X)`". Each class's C handler is written once; its 1,497 uses come free.
2. **Closures are tracked, not guessed.** Hand ports live in `src/closures_moves.c` and `src/closures_abilities.c`, each registered under the owner and source hash it was written against. A changed upstream closure therefore reads as unported instead of running stale logic. 735 upstream closures (move conditions, ability predicates) can't be dumped as data. They're listed in `gen/out/closures.txt` with a hash of their source. After an upstream bump, `tools/redump.sh` says exactly which ones changed. Values a closure captures from its constructor (the type a Blaze-like ability boosts, Rollout's limit, reset rules and combo moves) aren't in its source, so the dumper recovers them by calling the closure on probe inputs, and they become part of its hash and of the generated data (`pr_closure_info.cap` and `.vals`). That's the answer to "upstream updates must be ported by hand": the tool tells you what to re-port.
3. **Unported logic is loud.** Every upstream `applyAbAttrs` site calls `pr_apply_ab_attrs`, which runs the C handler for each attribute class and reports any class without one. Move attribute sites call `pr_hook_move`. If a case touches anything unported, it's logged as *not covered*, never counted as a pass. The difftest prints the top missing classes, which is the porting to-do list, ranked by how often each class matters.
4. **State that isn't modelled yet has one entry point.** Wave data (`pr_wave_ability_applied`) is a single function in `src/hooks.c`. Modifiers and then tags went through the same kind of entry point until they were ported, and replacing it made every caller correct at once. The caller passes how much of that state upstream holds; with none, "absent" is exact, and with some, the query reports itself unported. Turn history (move histories, attacks received, the last turn order) went through its length the same way and is now modelled: each list keeps its true length and its newest entries, and a read past the kept ones reports itself unported. State the turn engine will own is passed in by the caller for now and checked by the test: the parties, the Pokemon with a move still queued this turn, the turn history, the global RNG's state, and Super Fang's remembered HP.
5. **Same arithmetic as JS.** IEEE doubles everywhere upstream uses JS numbers, `-ffp-contract=off`, the same operation order, and V8's own `Math.pow` instead of the C library's. Names follow upstream (`pr_get_effective_stat` = `Pokemon.getEffectiveStat`) so each function diffs against its TS original.
6. **No platform code.** Plain C99; builds on the PC for tests and with devkitARM for the 3DS (checked: compiles warning-free with `-march=armv6k -mfloat-abi=hard`). On ARM the logic is about 50 KB of code and the data about 380 KB of read-only tables.

## Layout
- `include/pr/` public headers (`rng.h`, `battle.h`)
- `src/` ported logic (`rng.c`, `hooks.c`, `pokemon.c`, `damage.c`, `move_attrs.c`, `ab_attrs.c` ability dispatch and handlers, `modifiers.c` held items and other modifiers, `tags.c` battler and arena tags, `battler_tags.c` battler tags being added, lapsed and removed, `history.c` move histories, attacks received and the last turn order, `js_math.c` V8's `Math.pow`, `closures.c` registry, `closures_moves.c`, `closures_abilities.c` and `closures_conditions.c` hand-ported closures, `engine.c` the phase queue, `phases.c` the phases of a turn, `move_effects.c` move secondary effects)
- `gen/dump-upstream.test.ts` dumper (runs in an upstream checkout), `gen/upstream/*.json` dump (not in git; `tools/redump.sh` recreates it), `gen/gen_tables.py` generator, `gen/out/` generated C (committed so the code builds without upstream; do not edit), `gen/check_closures.py` drift report
- `tests/difftest.producer.test.ts` upstream half of the differential test; `tests/difftest.c` C half; `tests/turntest.producer.test.ts` and `tests/turntest.c` the whole-turn test (copy the producer to `test/tests/zz-dump/` in an upstream checkout; `TURN_OUT`, `TURN_N`, `TURN_SEED`, `TURN_RICH`, `TURN_COND`, `TURN_PROBE`, `TURN_STAT`, `TURN_STATUS`, `TURN_SPROBE`, `TURN_ONHIT`, `TURN_CHARGE`, `TURN_TAGS`, `TURN_HEAL`, `TURN_WEATHER`, `TURN_TRAPS`, `TURN_PROTECT`; `TURNTEST_WHY=<reason>` makes `turntest` list the cases a reason left out, `TURNTEST_COVERED=1` the covered ones); `tests/rng_*` RNG check
- `tools/setup-upstream.sh <dir>` clones and prepares upstream (no images or audio are fetched). `tools/difftest.sh <dir> [N] [seed]` runs a differential test. `tools/redump.sh <dir>` refreshes the data and reports closure drift.

Build: `make` (outputs to `build/`). `tools/closures.py` shows an owner's closures and fills registry hashes.

## Custom content (planned, not built)
Upstream ids are compiled into const tables. Custom species, moves, abilities and items will use a reserved id range (from 30000 up) and load from a small pack on the SD card at startup. They'll reuse the same attribute classes, so a custom move is data such as "StatStageChangeAttr {stats: [ATK], stages: 2}" plus, only if needed, a new C handler. Lookups already go through one function per table (`pr_pokemon_form`), which is where the custom registry plugs in.

## Known gaps in the tooling
- The dumper only reads enums from `src/enums/`; a few live elsewhere (e.g. `TerrainType` in `src/data/terrain.ts`), so those are written by hand for now (`include/pr/battle.h`).
- A closure's hash covers its own source and probed captures, not named helpers it calls (`anyTypeMoveConversionCondition`, `targetSleptOrComatoseCondition`). Check those by hand after an upstream bump.
