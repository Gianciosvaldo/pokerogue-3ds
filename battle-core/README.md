## Task 69: Weather-driven quiet form changes (2026-10-04)

Forecast, Flower Gift and Ice Face now dispatch the pinned weather/summon triggers into a queued `QuietFormChangePhase`. The queued target is selected before mutation. Execution preserves real identity and fusion selection, recalculates form stats and natural passives, applies the non-recursive post-form ability callbacks once per ability per turn, displays the pinned text directly, and removes Autotomize. Castform/Cherrim leaving-field resets retain delayed ordering; off-field enemies keep the upstream early exit. Switching a weather suppressor out dispatches to the other holders, excluding the newly summoned Pokemon as upstream does.

The additive producer invokes the actual pinned trigger selector, ability callbacks, arena weather replacement and quiet phase. Only insertion/advance and asset/UI boundaries are intercepted. Its nine modes cover weather/revert triggers, weather callbacks, summon callbacks, weather replacement, Cloud Nine suppression, field-wide restoration, inactive form reset, and weather-suppressor faint restoration. Loaded overrides and natural form-dependent passives are included. It compares queue targets before mutation and form/types/stats/HP/ability/weather/passive/message after the actual quiet phase; global RNG remains unchanged. It does not edit an established producer/comparator, enable natural Illusion, or claim hardware presentation or full run lifecycle parity.

Validation uses upstream `8555c08c823b856cbec4eb99ca84ea52a955836d`, Node 24.19.0 / Vitest 4.1.11, GCC 13.3. The differential test found real bugs, all fixed: Stellar Tera typing, Shedinja's forced Bug tera type, charging moves (Fly, Dive) not counting as attacks for Wonder Guard, and Disguise's recoil, which upstream deals in the middle of the damage calculation: when it knocks the Pokemon out, a passive checked right after (Fluffy, say) no longer applies. The port had put the recoil off until later; it now happens at once. Until this revision it also skipped every move whose listed power is -1 (35 moves, among them Low Kick, Grass Knot, Heavy Slam, Flail, Gyro Ball, Electro Ball, Punishment, Seismic Toss and Super Fang), so their code was unchecked even though an earlier version of this README counted them as verified. They're in the test now and match.

What the test doesn't cover yet: doubles and fusions. Code that is ported but can't show in this test: status moves hitting a Substitute and Prankster's priority (the test only uses damaging moves, and Prankster only speeds up status moves), Parental Bond's refusal of spread moves (no damaging move has two targets in a single battle), and the condition that turns off Color Change, Pickpocket, Berserk, Anger Shell, Wimp Out and Emergency Exit after a Sheer Force hit (it reads the attacks received and runs, since `hasAbilityWithAttr` and co. ask `canApplyAbility` before looking at the attributes, but none of those abilities has an attribute the damage path reads: 3 deliberate bugs in it change no answer). Side effects that belong to the turn engine (Volt Absorb healing, Sap Sipper's stat raise, Sturdy's tag, Disguise's form change, Present's heal, the stat boosts a boss gets when damage breaks one of its segments) are recorded as *deferred*: the damage result is checked, the side effect isn't applied until the phase engine exists, and nothing compares the deferred list with upstream (so Sturdy skipping its tag when it already has one is ported but unchecked). Of about 110,000 cases 6 stay uncovered: 4 where Disguise's recoil, which upstream deals in the middle of the damage calculation, lands on a Pokemon with Wimp Out or Emergency Exit (`PostDamageAbAttr`: the switch-out belongs to the turn engine), and 2 where Revenge or a Counter-like move scans more received attacks than the 8 kept without finding its answer. Parental Bond's second hit with Expanding Force or Tera Starstorm, whose target can change (`VariableTargetAttr`), is still unported too; it didn't come up in this revision's streams. The game-mode rules around passives (no passive for the classic final boss or endless bosses, the passives challenge, daily-run passives) aren't modelled: they belong to the mode layer.

Two things upstream does that a reader might not expect, both kept: `getAttackDamage` doesn't cap damage at the target's HP, and with the enemy's damage tokens (up to 999 stacks of x1.05 in endless mode) it can exceed 2^63, so damage is a double in the C port too. And the JS `Math.pow` result depends on the engine: the reference here is Node 22 (V8 12.4), whose pow is fdlibm's; newer V8 versions switched to a correctly rounded pow, so upstream itself can deal 1 point more or less on a different browser.

## Turn engine (in progress, 2026-09-27)
`src/engine.c` ports upstream's phase queue: the `PhaseTree` levels, deferral, and the dynamic speed-ordered queues with their markers (MovePhase's own queue included), so a phase lands where upstream would put it even when it only shows something. `src/phases.c` ports the phases of a turn: TurnStart, Move, MoveEffect, MoveEnd, Faint, CheckInterlude, PositionalTag, Berry, CheckStatusEffect, PostTurnStatusEffect, TurnEnd, ResetStatus, StatStageChange, ObtainStatusEffect and MoveCharge, plus the message, animation and ability-display phases. A phase with no port yet is still created where upstream creates it, and the turn is reported unported. `src/move_effects.c` has the gates in front of a move's secondary effects (the effect chance and its roll), the stat changes moves make and the statuses they inflict. `src/closures_conditions.c` has the moves' own failure conditions (`move.applyConditions`): 79 of the 83 distinct sources, plus Belch's use of the one it shares with Stuff Cheeks, including Protect's consecutive-use odds, Fake Out, the weather and terrain moves, OHKO moves with Sturdy, screens, heal moves, Counter, Disable, Encore, Last Resort, Destiny Bond, Damp and Teleport. Not ported: Transform, Sketch, Stuff Cheeks (berries aren't a ported class) and Revival Blessing. Some ported ones still report themselves unported in a state they don't model: the switch-out check of Roar, Whirlwind, Teleport, Baton Pass and Shed Tail, Chilly Reception in snow, an entry hazard already down, Reflect Type on a typeless target, Destiny Bond on wave 200 or a multiple of 250 (the game mode decides), and Rototiller with a bench.

The stat-change phase is complete as upstream has it: Simple and Contrary, Mist (and Infiltrator getting through it), the abilities that stop drops (Clear Body, White Smoke, Full Metal Body, Hyper Cutter, Keen Eye, Big Pecks, Illuminate, Mind's Eye, Flower Veil on the user's side), Mirror Armor reflecting drops back, raises and drops split into two phases, the clamp to ±6 and its messages, Opportunist copying raises, Defiant and Competitive. The White Herb check only reports itself (held items aren't in the turn test yet). The move conditions on stat changes are ported too (Rototiller, Flower Shield, Venom Drench, Magnetic Flux, Gear Up, Tera Blast's Stellar drop). Arena tags now count down at the end of the turn and end with their message; the four whose lapse or removal does more (Imprison, the Fire/Grass Pledge sea of fire, pending heals, Neutralizing Gas ending) report themselves unported. Status conditions are complete for moves that inflict them (the 86 moves with `StatusEffectAttr`, Tri Attack and Dire Claw picking theirs with the global RNG before the chance roll): `canSetStatus` with an existing or pending status, Misty Terrain on a grounded target, the type immunities (Poison and Steel unless the source has Corrosion, Electric, Ice or sun for freezing, Fire, Electric Terrain for sleep), the Pokemon's own abilities (Limber, Insomnia, Water Veil, Leaf Guard, Comatose, Shields Down in Meteor Form...), its side's (Sweet Veil, Pastel Veil, Flower Veil) and Safeguard with Infiltrator; then the ObtainStatusEffectPhase with its sleep length roll and Synchronize. Over a turn: sleep counting down (Early Bird, Sleep Talk and Snore), freezing with the one-in-four thaw and the moves that thaw their user (Burn Up only for a Fire type), full paralysis one time in eight, and poison, bad poison and burn damage at the end of the turn (Magic Guard, Poison Heal blocking it, Heatproof). A Pokemon with a cure-on-entry ability (Limber, Insomnia, Water Veil...) that has the status anyway is cured after being hit. Upstream keeps the last status each of those abilities cured on the ability object itself, for the whole session, and the cure message only appears once that is set; the C keeps the same session-wide table (`pr_status_healed_*`), and the test writes it with the state. Atk at +6 or both at -6, a Shed Tail user at exactly half of an odd maximum HP, rounded up).

## Design (the decisions that make 180k lines tractable)
1. **Data is generated, never hand-typed.** Upstream builds moves and abilities from ~430 attribute classes (`StatStageChangeAttr`, `StatMultiplierAbAttr`...). The dumper serialises every instance with its parameters. `gen/gen_tables.py` emits one C struct per class, a const table of instances, and an is-a bitmap for the class hierarchy. Upstream's `applyAbAttrs("X")` becomes "iterate attrs where `pr_attr_is(cls, PR_AC_X)`". Each class's C handler is written once; its 1,497 uses come free.
2. **Closures are tracked, not guessed.** Hand ports live in `src/closures_moves.c` and `src/closures_abilities.c`, each registered under the owner and source hash it was written against. A changed upstream closure therefore reads as unported instead of running stale logic. 735 upstream closures (move conditions, ability predicates) can't be dumped as data. They're listed in `gen/out/closures.txt` with a hash of their source. After an upstream bump, `tools/redump.sh` says exactly which ones changed. Values a closure captures from its constructor (the type a Blaze-like ability boosts, Rollout's limit, reset rules and combo moves) aren't in its source, so the dumper recovers them by calling the closure on probe inputs, and they become part of its hash and of the generated data (`pr_closure_info.cap` and `.vals`). That's the answer to "upstream updates must be ported by hand": the tool tells you what to re-port.
3. **Unported logic is loud.** Every upstream `applyAbAttrs` site calls `pr_apply_ab_attrs`, which runs the C handler for each attribute class and reports any class without one. Move attribute sites call `pr_hook_move`. If a case touches anything unported, it's logged as *not covered*, never counted as a pass. The difftest prints the top missing classes, which is the porting to-do list, ranked by how often each class matters.
4. **State that isn't modelled yet has one entry point.** Wave data (`pr_wave_ability_applied`) is a single function in `src/hooks.c`. Modifiers and then tags went through the same kind of entry point until they were ported, and replacing it made every caller correct at once. The caller passes how much of that state upstream holds; with none, "absent" is exact, and with some, the query reports itself unported. Turn history (move histories, attacks received, the last turn order) went through its length the same way and is now modelled: each list keeps its true length and its newest entries, and a read past the kept ones reports itself unported. State the turn engine will own is passed in by the caller for now and checked by the test: the parties, the Pokemon with a move still queued this turn, the turn history, the global RNG's state, and Super Fang's remembered HP.
5. **Same arithmetic as JS.** IEEE doubles everywhere upstream uses JS numbers, `-ffp-contract=off`, the same operation order, and V8's own `Math.pow` instead of the C library's. Names follow upstream (`pr_get_effective_stat` = `Pokemon.getEffectiveStat`) so each function diffs against its TS original.
6. **No platform code.** Plain C99; builds on the PC for tests and with devkitARM for the 3DS (checked: compiles warning-free with `-march=armv6k -mfloat-abi=hard`). On ARM the logic is about 50 KB of code and the data about 380 KB of read-only tables.

## Layout
- `include/pr/` public headers (`rng.h`, `battle.h`)
- `src/` ported logic (`rng.c`, `hooks.c`, `pokemon.c`, `damage.c`, `move_attrs.c`, `ab_attrs.c` ability dispatch and handlers, `modifiers.c` held items and other modifiers, `tags.c` battler and arena tags, `battler_tags.c` battler tags being added, lapsed and removed, `history.c` move histories, attacks received and the last turn order, `js_math.c` V8's `Math.pow`, `closures.c` registry, `closures_moves.c`, `closures_abilities.c` and `closures_conditions.c` hand-ported closures, `engine.c` the phase queue, `phases.c` the phases of a turn, `move_effects.c` move secondary effects, `called_moves.c` the moves that call moves and target selection)
- `gen/dump-upstream.test.ts` dumper (runs in an upstream checkout), `gen/upstream/*.json` dump (not in git; `tools/redump.sh` recreates it), `gen/gen_tables.py` generator, `gen/out/` generated C (committed so the code builds without upstream; do not edit), `gen/check_closures.py` drift report
- `tests/difftest.producer.test.ts` upstream half of the differential test; `tests/difftest.c` C half; `tests/turntest.producer.test.ts` and `tests/turntest.c` the whole-turn test (copy the producer to `test/tests/zz-dump/` in an upstream checkout; `TURN_OUT`, `TURN_N`, `TURN_SEED`, `TURN_RICH`, `TURN_COND`, `TURN_PROBE`, `TURN_STAT`, `TURN_STATUS`, `TURN_SPROBE`, `TURN_ONHIT`, `TURN_CHARGE`, `TURN_TAGS`, `TURN_HEAL`, `TURN_WEATHER`, `TURN_BOOST`, `TURN_TRAPS`, `TURN_PROTECT`, `TURN_ARENA`, `TURN_EFFECTS`, `TURN_CALLED`, `TURN_HEADER`, `TURN_DELAYED`, `TURN_CPROBE`, `TURN_START`, `TURN_TRAINER`; `TURNTEST_WHY=<reason>` makes `turntest` list the cases a reason left out, `TURNTEST_COVERED=1` the covered ones); `tests/rng_*` RNG check

Build: `make` (outputs to `build/`). `tools/closures.py` shows an owner's closures and fills registry hashes.

## Custom content (planned, not built)
Upstream ids are compiled into const tables. Custom species, moves, abilities and items will use a reserved id range (from 30000 up) and load from a small pack on the SD card at startup. They'll reuse the same attribute classes, so a custom move is data such as "StatStageChangeAttr {stats: [ATK], stages: 2}" plus, only if needed, a new C handler. Lookups already go through one function per table (`pr_pokemon_form`), which is where the custom registry plugs in.

## Known gaps in the tooling
- The dumper only reads enums from `src/enums/`; a few live elsewhere (e.g. `TerrainType` in `src/data/terrain.ts`), so those are written by hand for now (`include/pr/battle.h`).
- A closure's hash covers its own source and probed captures, not named helpers it calls (`anyTypeMoveConversionCondition`, `targetSleptOrComatoseCondition`). Check those by hand after an upstream bump.
