# Owning single/double summon continuation

Reference: upstream `8555c08c823b856cbec4eb99ca84ea52a955836d`, locales
`270ed0a2938b2c3e2e34e7de940e84aecadf548b`, Node22.23.3.

## Fixed behavior

When SummonPhase replaces a fainted party slot with an already-out ally, source
getField immediately reflects the changed party order. C previously updated
only the receiving field slot, leaving the other slot pointing at the same
Pokemon. The swap now refreshes all field references on that side. Both player
and enemy first-slot replacements are covered. The blanket doubles marker on
this summon path is removed after comparing the real owning phase. Other
unsupported ability/form/item contexts retain their own accounting.

## Actual source execution and comparison

`tests/summon-entry.producer.test.ts` invokes the real SummonPhase.start through
preSummon, summon, modifier/stat refresh, fieldSetup and onEnd. A naturally
created double battle supplies three players and three enemies including a
reserve. A real Youngster supplies trainer names. Visual/audio/UI callback
completion is intercepted; gameplay, stat calculation, party swaps and form
hooks remain native. BattleScene's original RNG method is captured before
GameManager installs its maximum-roll stub and restored after startup.

The final version3 observations include explicit shiny input, stale nature/stat
cache controls and nonempty prior-turn hit counts. Version1 omitted the shiny
input and is historical only; its mismatch count is not a runtime-bug count.
Version2 establishes the simpler owning boundary; version3 is the stronger
completion evidence. No expected values are regenerated by the transport.

The replay checks all six actors' identities, species/form/level/nature/IVs,
HP/stats, ability, shiny/on-field/switching flags, summon/turn/wave/hit counters,
complete party order and all four field references. It also compares exact
localized send-out messages, every static queue level, all dynamic marker types
and payloads/constructor arguments, and both RNG streams.

## Remaining boundaries

The source fixtures use ordinary non-fused Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle and
Magikarp, no held inventory, no status and NONE abilities. This does not prove
all PreSummon/form/hazard callbacks, Commander/Dondozo partner lifecycle,
missing/illegal/no-living-party recovery, loaded SummonPhase behavior or mystery
summonWild execution. The controller fixture is not a legal Classic-clear proof.

Doubles command/target handling, natural run/app doubles, double save/recovery,
Eternatus encounter-end locking/IV Scanner integration and wide-level Endless
remain separate work. No run-core edits or hardware/emulator acceptance.
