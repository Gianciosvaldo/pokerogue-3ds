# Ordinary trainer constructor continuation

Pinned upstream: `8555c08c823b856cbec4eb99ca84ea52a955836d`.
Pinned locales: `270ed0a2938b2c3e2e34e7de940e84aecadf548b`.

Ordinary Classic trainers below level 25 now generate their weighted moveset and
finish their constructor inside the member's `executeWithSeedOffset` stream.
Previously game-core generated a last-four-level-moves stand-in and ran the
constructor finish after run-core had restored the ambient RNG. This changes
moves, trainer IVs and the subsequent encounter RNG to the source order for the
owned domain. Young Couple's two native random-pool factories are also ported;
the three ordinary paired classes are enabled for ordinary encounters.

The new optional run-core continuation leaves the existing prefix-only API
intact. A declining continuation restores its temporary rolls and RNG. Rivals
retain their separate deferred constructor owner. Game-core stages completed
members in static storage and publishes them only after the party prefix
succeeds, preserving the ARM stack budget and avoiding a partial live party.

## Source boundaries

Read directly in the pinned checkout:

- `src/field/trainer.ts`, `Trainer.genPartyMember`: every real base constructor,
  moveset and finish runs inside the per-member seed offset; the ambient RNG is
  restored afterward. Same-seed and member-specific offsets both matter.
- `src/data/trainers/trainer-config.ts`: the exact Young Couple pools, explicit
  partner slot and same-seed configuration; Twins and Mysterious Sisters remain
  covered by their retained independent factory proof.
- `src/ai/ai-moveset-gen.ts`: trainer relearner inclusion, superseded-move
  filtering, OHKO exclusion, common trainer/boss blocklists, self-KO/buff/multiturn
  weights and per-slot damage-type diversity. Trainer and boss hard exclusions
  differ, so trainer self-KO moves and Pain Split are not globally removed.
- `src/data/balance/moves/moveset-generation.ts`: common TMs begin at 25,
  relearner moves at 40 and egg moves at 80. The thresholds are dumped from the
  source alongside the move facts; the C owner rejects the higher domain.
- `src/field/pokemon.ts`: trainer IVs are rerolled after moveset generation.
  Move weights use the initial constructor IVs/stats. A later no-item
  `BattleScene.updateModifiers(false)` refresh uses the final IVs.

Two new move facts are generated by the real native move attributes:
`StatStageChangeAttr` with `selfTarget && stages > 1`, and `OneHitKOAttr`.
The generated power tables and existing wild rules remain shared.

## Actual native observations

`tests/trainer-constructor.producer.test.ts` captures native
`BattleScene.prototype.randBattleSeedInt` **before** constructing `GameManager`
and restores it after starting the battle. It checks that the native base/enemy
moveset and effective-power methods are real and unchanged by the framework.
Wrappers observe calls to the real `Trainer.genPartyMember`,
`addEnemyPokemon` and `generateAndPopulateMoveset`; they invoke the real methods.
There is no C-derived expected-state model or replacement moveset algorithm.

The committed compressed fixture retains all 696 previous first-member
observations unchanged: Youngster/Breeder at levels 5/24/25/69/70/100 and waves
11/99, plus all 63 ordinary classes at levels 5/24 and waves 11/99. It then adds
504 complete native parties containing 1,184 members, including 48 double
parties. Real prior members are placed into the source party before generating
the next member, exercising duplicate roots, balancing and same-species rules.
Native template metadata, constructor inputs/outputs, moves/PP, all four RNG
fields and the final no-item stat refresh are transported into the C fixture.
Trainer IDs are controlled at 12345/54321; biome/weather/terrain are Town/none/none,
with the observed wave-cycle offset recorded per case and timed events inactive.

Production comparison results:

- 1,880 exact base prefixes; 1,752 exact moveset/PP/constructor-finish streams.
- All 504 complete parties match member order, partner slots, identity,
  abilities, IVs, friendship, shiny metadata, refreshed stats and HP.
- 128 higher-level first-member cases retain prefix coverage and reject the
  weighted factory without changing the Pokémon or RNG.
- 20 rejection cases cover unsupported roles, levels, modes, challenge/fusion/
  boss/passive/Tera/tag states, occupied field/moveset and form callbacks,
  including an injected unsupported stat operation. A second-member constructor
  failure leaves the live party unchanged and restores the ambient RNG.
- 120,685 assertions pass. The same test passes with AddressSanitizer,
  UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer and LeakSanitizer on the changed production owners
  and comparison runner; other linked library objects are not instrumented.
- A fresh native capture and its C projection are byte-identical to the frozen
  fixture. The transport rejects 14 malformed or forged captures. Three pristine
  compiled-owner controls pass; 14 compiled production faults are rejected.
- Retained ordinary-wild source proof passes all 1,613 cases / 168,244 assertions.
  Existing Challengers, pair-factory, battle/app/custom/save and ARM build checks
  remain required; no coverage floor or Task63 policy changes.

Reproduce with Node 22 and the prepared pinned checkout:

```sh
make -C game-core trainer-constructor-test trainer-constructor-capture trainer-constructor-faults
bash game-core/tools/reproduce-trainer-constructors.sh /path/to/pinned-upstream /tmp/trainer-native
```

Only the upstream assets **gitlink** is pinned by these checks; an actual hardware
asset decode is not established by headless source observations.

## Still open

This is bounded constructor coverage, not battle-core completion. Higher-level
TM/egg pools and caps, trainer-specific signature policies, rivals/Gym/E4 full
movesets, instant Tera, trainer fusions, challenges/Daily/custom constructors and
post-moveset form callbacks remain outside this owner. Full encounter held-item
rolls and played battles have their separate retained proofs. Broader source
closure coverage and integrated wide-level Endless review are still required.
No Windows builder is produced by this change.
